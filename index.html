Needs vs Wants Diagnostic Matrix

Benchmark every outflow against 14 behavioral criteria to eliminate financial leaks before authorizing any transaction.

✓ Present / Active | ∅ Null / Inapplicable
Evaluation Property Essential Needs Optional Wants Diagnostic Evaluation Rule Status Tier
1. Physiological Survival Support ✓ ∅ Sustains baseline biological life, hydration, and safe lodging. MANDATORY
2. 24-Hour Postponability ∅ ✓ Can be delayed 24–48 hours without causing structural distress. FLEXIBLE
3. Structural Penalty on Omission ✓ ∅ Failure to clear creates failed grades, legal penalties, or eviction. CRITICAL
4. Debt Financing Eligibility ✓ ∅ Permitted solely in verified health emergencies with confirmed repayment. RESTRICTED
5. Zero-Cost Free Substitutes Available ∅ ✓ Borrowing event fits, using campus library books, public open Wi-Fi. SUBSTITUTABLE
6. Post-Purchase Buyer's Remorse Risk ∅ ✓ High probability of regret once initial purchase excitement fades. BEHAVIORAL
7. Threatens Emergency Survival Buffer ∅ ✓ Invades untouchable reserve capital to fund temporary desires. HIGH RISK
8. Silent Recurring Subscription Risk ∅ ✓ Unused entertainment and premium trials renewing in the background. LEAKAGE
9. Immediate Resale Depreciation ∅ ✓ Loses 30–70% of market value immediately upon retail purchase. DEPRECIATES
10. Expands Future Earning Power ✓ ∅ Productive tooling, skill workshops, core textbooks, study laptops. PRODUCTIVE
11. Driven by Peer Pressure / Validation ∅ ✓ Purchasing to match peer lifestyle expectations or social media image. STATUS DEBT
12. Cost-Effective in Bulk Units ✓ ∅ Non-perishable grains, toiletries, study stationery yield unit savings. UNIT VALUE
13. Inelastic Demand Under Inflation ✓ ∅ Must be procured regardless of price surges; demands strict budgeting. INELASTIC
14. Direct Budget Allocation Target ✓ ✓ 50–60% allocated to Needs; 15–20% max allocated to Wants. BALANCED
